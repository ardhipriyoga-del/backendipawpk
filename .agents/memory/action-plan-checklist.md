---
name: Action plan checklist
description: Rule connecting inpatient action plans to next-day billing checklist review.
---

An action plan date is stored per episode and makes the patient eligible for the action-billing checklist starting the day after the planned action.

**Why:** Admission date and procedure date are different operational triggers. A patient admitted today may still need billing review tomorrow because of a planned procedure.

**How to apply:** Keep the action date alongside checklist episode answers, never overwrite the core patient record, expose a card-level action button, and require the conditional “Billing Tindakan Sudah Dicek” item before completing that checklist cycle.
